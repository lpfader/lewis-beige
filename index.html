About This is a project in the style of low-fi 2-bit dot-matrix. I have a lot of photoshop projects and photography demonstrations but since the photography is mostly film (35mm & 120mm) and the photoshop is for work (e.g. my resume and backgrounds for MLTC & MSAA) I want to combine my photography with photoshop.

What are we looking at? Hover over the thumbnails to enlarge them. I may end up moving in a different direction but for now I want this to be a simple way to be a bit creative while demonstrating my web development capabilities.
